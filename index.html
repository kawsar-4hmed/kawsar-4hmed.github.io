01About me
MD. Kawsar Ahmed

Hi, I'm Kawsar. I recently completed my Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), Bangladesh. I'm drawn to problems where physics, computation, and data meet — and I try to build a real, working understanding of a system before I try to optimize it.

I'm currently working on hydrogen–diesel dual-fuel combustion — using CFD simulation and machine learning to understand how engines burn fuel and produce emissions, then searching for operating conditions that improve efficiency without making emissions worse. This work has grown into several research manuscripts and conference papers I'm developing, bringing together the two things I care most about: combustion and thermal-fluid science, and using data-driven methods to make sense of complex systems.

I'm now looking for graduate research opportunities where I can take this further. This combustion and emissions work is just where I'm currently focused — my broader interests span biofuels, renewable energy, automotive engineering, fuel systems, fluid dynamics, and aerodynamics — really, any problem where thermal-fluid science meets computation.
