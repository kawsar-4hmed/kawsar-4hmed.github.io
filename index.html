01About me
MD. Kawsar Ahmed

MD. Kawsar Ahmed is a Mechanical Engineering student at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), Bangladesh, working at the intersection of combustion engineering and data-driven optimization.

His research centers on hydrogen–diesel dual-fuel engines — using numerical simulation to understand combustion and emission behavior, then applying machine learning to find operating conditions that improve efficiency while cutting emissions.

Comfortable moving between simulation software, numerical methods, and Python-based ML pipelines, with an interest in surrogate modeling and multi-objective optimization for engineering systems.
